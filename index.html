更年期，是一段過渡，不是妳的全部。Menopause is a transition—not the whole story of who you are.

更年期是女性生命中自然的生理階段。卵巢功能與荷爾蒙分泌逐漸改變，月經可能先變得不規律，也可能伴隨潮熱、夜間盜汗、睡眠困擾、情緒波動或泌尿生殖系統不適。每個人的經驗都不同，有些人症狀很輕，有些人則需要醫療協助。妳不必把不舒服當成「只能忍耐」的事，也不需要把所有變化都歸咎於更年期。Menopause is a natural stage of life. As ovarian function and hormone levels change, periods may become irregular. Some people also experience hot flashes, night sweats, sleep problems, mood changes, or genitourinary symptoms. Everyone's experience is different: symptoms may be mild, or they may benefit from medical care. You do not have to simply endure discomfort, and not every change should automatically be attributed to menopause.

01

圍絕經期Perimenopause

月經週期可能出現變化，身體感受也可能逐漸不同。這段時間長短因人而異。Menstrual cycles may change, and you may begin to notice new physical experiences. The length of this transition varies.

02

停經Menopause

停經通常是回顧性判定：連續 12 個月沒有月經，且沒有其他明確原因。Menopause is generally identified retrospectively after 12 consecutive months without a period, when no other cause explains it.

03

停經後期Postmenopause

進入停經後，仍可持續關注骨骼、心血管、睡眠與泌尿生殖健康，並與醫療人員討論個人需求。After menopause, continue to care for bone, cardiovascular, sleep, and genitourinary health, and discuss your individual needs with a healthcare professional.

情緒照顧小練習Small Practices for Emotional Wellbeing

🌬️ 容易煩躁時When You Feel Irritable

先暫停幾分鐘，辨認自己是生氣、委屈、疲憊，還是感到不被理解。等情緒稍緩，再決定如何回應。Pause for a few minutes. Notice whether you feel angry, hurt, exhausted, or unheard. When the feeling settles, decide how you would like to respond.

🌙 睡不好的時候When Sleep Is Difficult

盡量維持規律起床時間、白天接觸自然光，記錄夜醒與潮熱情況。持續困擾可向醫療人員詢問失眠治療選項。Keep a regular wake-up time, get daylight during the day, and track night waking and hot flashes. Ask a healthcare professional about treatment options if sleep problems persist.

💚 留一點時間給自己Make Time for Yourself

散步、聽音樂、和信任的人聊天，或重新拾起一項喜歡的活動。照顧自己不是自私。Take a walk, listen to music, talk with someone you trust, or return to an activity you enjoy. Taking care of yourself is not selfish.

和家人好好說，也讓自己被聽見Communicate with Family—and Let Yourself Be Heard

☕ 和伴侶溝通Talking with Your Partner

「我最近睡眠不太好，白天容易疲憊。我希望你先聽聽我的感受，也和我一起分擔家裡的事情。」“I have not been sleeping well lately, and I feel tired during the day. I hope you can listen to how I feel and share household responsibilities with me.”

🌱 和子女溝通Talking with Your Children

「媽媽最近身體和情緒有些變化，我也在學習照顧自己。如果我們都有情緒，可以先停一下，平靜後再談。」“My body and emotions have been changing lately, and I am learning how to care for myself. If either of us feels upset, we can take a break and talk when we feel calmer.”
